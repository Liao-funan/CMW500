CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:THRoughput:WINDow <Size>

Configures the number of subframes on the X-axis of the throughput diagram (duration of a single-shot measurement).

The size cannot be smaller than the update interval.

Parameters:
<Size>
Range: 200  to  120000
*RST:60000
