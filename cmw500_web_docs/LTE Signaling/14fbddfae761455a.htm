CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:CONNection:SCC<c>:NOLayers <Number>

Selects the number of layers for MIMO 4x4 with spatial multiplexing (TM 3 and 4).

Suffix: 
<c>
1..7
Parameters:
<Number>
L2 | L4

Two layers or four layers

*RST:Depends on available options
