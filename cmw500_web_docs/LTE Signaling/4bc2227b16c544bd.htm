CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>[:PCC]:DMODe <Mode>
CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:SCC<c>:DMODe <Mode>

Selects the duplex mode of the LTE signal: FDD or TDD.

See also CONFigure:​LTE:​SIGN<i>[:​PCC]:​DMODe:​UCSPecific.

1..4

<Mode>
