CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:CONNection:CDRX:LDCYcle <Cycle>

Configures the duration of one long DRX cycle. If short DRX cycles are enabled, the long DRX cycle duration must be divisible by the short DRX cycle duration.

Parameters:
<Cycle>
SF10 | SF20 | SF32 | SF40 | SF60 | SF64 | SF70 | SF80 | SF128 | SF160 | SF256 | SF320 | SF512 | SF640 | SF1024 | SF1280 | SF2048 | SF2560 | SF5120 | SF10240

SFn means n subframes

*RST:SF40
Example: 

See "Configuring Connected DRX"

Firmware/Software: 

V3.2.20

V3.7.10: SF60, SF70, SF5120, SF10240

Options: R&S CMW-KS590 for SF5120, SF10240
Manual operation: See "Long DRX Cycle"
