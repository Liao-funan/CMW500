CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:CONNection:DLPadding <Value>

Activates or deactivates downlink padding at the MAC layer (filling an allocated RMC with padding bits when no data is available from higher layers).

Parameters:
<Value>
OFF | ON
*RST:ON
