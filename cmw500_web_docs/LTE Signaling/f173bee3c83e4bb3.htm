CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:CONNection:CDRX:IMODe:PTWindow <Window>

Configures the duration of one paging time window in idle mode.

Parameters:
<Window>
W1280 | W2560 | W3840 | W5120 | W6400 | W7680 | W8960 | W10240 | W11520 | W12800 | W14080 | W15360 | W16640 | W17920 | W19200 | W20480

Wn means n subframes

*RST:W128
