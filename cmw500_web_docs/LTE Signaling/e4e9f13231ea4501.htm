CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:CONNection:CDRX:RTIMer <Timer>

Configures the "drx-RetransmissionTimer".

Parameters:
<Timer>
PSF0 | PSF1 | PSF2 | PSF4 | PSF6 | PSF8 | PSF16 | PSF24 | PSF33 | PSF40 | PSF64 | PSF80 | PSF96 | PSF112 | PSF128 | PSF160 | PSF320

PSFn means n PDCCH subframes

*RST:PSF16

See "Configuring Connected DRX"

Firmware/Software:
