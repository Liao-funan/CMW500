RLC Throughput Measurement

The RLC throughput measurement is installed together with the "LTE Signaling" application. To access the measurement, press the softkey "LTE RX Meas" in the LTE signaling main view. Then select the tab "RLC Throughput".

The RLC throughput measurement evaluates the total data throughput in the downlink and in the uplink.
