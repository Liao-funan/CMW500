Prerequisites for Scenarios

Which scenarios are offered for selection, depends on the available software and hardware options. The dependencies are described in this section.

General hints:
  • Single-CMW setups
    In a single CMW, you can equip up to 4 TX/RX and up to 4 SUW or up to 2 SUA. If these resources are sufficient for a scenario, you can execute the scenario on a single CMW. But you can also use the scenario with a multi-CMW setup.
  • Multi-CMW setups
    For scenarios needing more hardware, you need a multi-CMW setup.
    The instruments are controlled by an R&S CMWC. The test software including the LTE signaling application is installed and executed on the R&S CMWC.
    The options can be distributed over several instruments. But the signaling units (SUA or SUW), TX/RX modules and I/Q boards for each carrier must be in the same instrument. Example: For one carrier, you cannot use a signaling unit of one instrument plus the TX/RX modules of another instrument.
    Coprocessing SUAs do not belong to a specific carrier and can be located in any instrument.
    The SW licenses are pooled in the R&S CMWC, so it does not matter where they are installed.
  • Co-location
    Some scenarios support co-location. That means you can use the same TX module for two carriers, if you put the carriers into the same band within a bandwidth of 80 MHz. If the required number of resources differs without and with co-location, both numbers are listed in column "TX" of the following table.
  • SUA vs. SUW
    Each SUA board provides two SUA partitions. The GUI and the remote commands select SUA partitions, not SUA boards. In one CMW, you have up to four SUA partitions or up to four SUW.
    For fading with the SUW, you need an I/Q board. For fading with the SUA, you need additional SUA resources.
    You can combine SUA and SUW in a single CMW, but one scenario uses only SUW or only SUA, not both in parallel. A CMW with SUA cannot contain I/Q boards.

All options listed in the following tables are R&S CMW-... options. Example: KS510 means R&S CMW-KS510. All scenarios require KS500 for FDD and KS550 for TDD in addition to the listed options. KS502/552 means KS502 for FDD or KS552 for TDD.

1) Required TX modules. "x/y" means "without co-location"/"with co-location". The number of required RX modules <n> is not listed. It equals the number of carriers (scenario "<n>CC..."). Exception: 4 RX required for "5CC - 1x1 1x1 1x1 1x1 1x1".

2) Number of required SUA partitions. "x/y" means "without coprocessing"/"with coprocessing". Coprocessing can be not possible "x/-", optional to reach the maximum throughput "x/y", or mandatory "-/y".

In general, each carrier needs one SUA partition. Exceptions:
  • If a carrier uses MIMO nx4, the carrier needs two SUA partitions (considered in the table).
  • If sum(carriers) = nx10, the PCC needs two SUA partitions (considered in the table).
  • If sum(carriers) < nx12 and CC ≥ 5, the PCC needs two SUA partitions (considered in the table).
  • If sum(carriers) ≥ nx12, coprocessing is mandatory (two SUA partitions more, considered in the table).
  • If an uplink uses 64-QAM, the carrier needs two SUA partitions. Possibly, you need more SUA partitions than listed in the table.

3) Number of required SUW. n/a means that the scenario is not supported with SUW.

KS502/552, KS512, KS520, KE500

1) Required TX modules. The number of required RX modules is ≤ the number of required TX modules.

2) Number of SUA partitions required for the unfaded carriers ("SUA for CC") plus number of SUA partitions required for fading ("SUA fading"). You need the sum of both columns. n/a means that the scenario is not supported with SUA, only with SUW.

The SUA supports extended fading. It does not support fading for MIMO 8x2.

For "SUA for CC", "x/y" means "without coprocessing"/"with coprocessing". Coprocessing can be not possible "x/-", optional to reach the maximum throughput "x/y", or mandatory "-/y".

In general, you need one SUA partition for each unfaded carrier plus one SUA partition for fading of the carrier. Exceptions:
  • If a carrier uses MIMO nx4, you need two partitions for the carrier and two partitions for fading (considered in the table).
  • If a carrier uses MIMO 8x2, you need two partitions for the carrier and two partitions for fading. Possibly, you need more partitions than listed in the table.
  • For more than 4 CC, the unfaded PCC needs two SUA partitions (considered in the table).
  • If the sum of the carriers is exactly nx10, the unfaded PCC needs two SUA partitions (considered in the table).
  • If the sum of the carriers is ≥ nx12, coprocessing is mandatory (two additional SUA partitions, considered in the table).
  • If an uplink uses 64-QAM, the unfaded carrier needs two partitions. Possibly, you need more partitions than listed in the table.

3) Number of required SUW. n/a means that the scenario is not supported with SUW.

4) Number of required I/Q boards and in brackets the board type. The first I/Q board of an instrument is R&S CMW-B510F, the second board is R&S CMW-B520F. Up to two DL TX antennas per carrier, the I/Q board supports extended fading. For more antennas, it supports standard fading.

For further reference:
  • "Internal Fading"
  • "Fading Simulator" (extended vs. standard fading)
