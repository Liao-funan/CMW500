LTE Signaling > Command Reference > Connection Control and States > Handover Preparation > PREPare:LTE:SIGN<i>:HANDover
PREPare:LTE:SIGN<i>:HANDover <Band>, <DLChannel>, <DLBandwidth>, <AddSpecEmission>

Configures the destination parameters for an intra-RAT handover within the LTE signaling application.

The duplex mode of the destination is the same as the duplex mode of the source. For a handover with duplex mode change, see PREPare:​LTE:​SIGN<i>:​HANDover:​ENHanced.

Parameters:
<Band>
FDD: UDEFined | OB1 | ... | OB28 | OB30 | OB31 | OB65 | OB66 | OB68 | OB70 | OB71

TDD: UDEFined | OB33 | ... | OB45 | OB46 | OB48 | OB250

Operating band of the handover destination

<DLChannel>

DL channel number valid for the selected operating band. The related UL channel number is calculated and set automatically.

For channel numbers depending on operating bands, see "Operating Bands".

Range: depends on operating band
<DLBandwidth>
B014 | B030 | B050 | B100 | B150 | B200

DL cell bandwidth (also used for UL)

1.4 MHz, 3 MHz, 5 MHz, 10 MHz, 15 MHz, 20 MHz

*RST:B100
<AddSpecEmission>
NS01 | ... | NS32

Value signaled to the UE as additional ACLR and spectrum emission requirement

*RST:NS01
