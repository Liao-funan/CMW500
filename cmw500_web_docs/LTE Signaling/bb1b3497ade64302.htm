STReam<s> and DL<s>

Commands that contain a STReam<s> or a DL<s> mnemonic are related to a single downlink stream.

The suffix <s> selects the downlink stream and is only relevant for scenarios with MIMO. For SISO scenarios, you can omit the suffix.
