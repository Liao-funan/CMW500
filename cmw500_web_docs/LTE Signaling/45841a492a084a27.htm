CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:CONNection:CDRX:ODTimer <Timer>

Configures the onDurationTimer. The value must be smaller than or equal to the long DRX cycle duration.

Parameters:
<Timer>
PSF1 | PSF2 | PSF3 | PSF4 | PSF5 | PSF6 | PSF8 | PSF10 | PSF20 | PSF30 | PSF40 | PSF50 | PSF60 | PSF80 | PSF100 | PSF200 | PSF300 | PSF400 | PSF500 | PSF600 | PSF800 | PSF1000 | PSF1200 | PSF1600

PSFn means n PDCCH subframes

*RST:PSF2

See "Configuring Connected DRX"

Firmware/Software: 

V3.2.20, V3.7.10: PSF300 to PSF1600
