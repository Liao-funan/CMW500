CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:CELL:TIME:SATTach <Enable>

Specifies whether the date and time information is sent to the UE during the attach procedure or not.

Parameters:
<Enable>
OFF | ON

ON: send date and time at attach

OFF: do not send date and time at attach

*RST:OFF
Example: 

See "Sending Date and Time Information to the UE"
