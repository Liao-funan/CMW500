LAA UE Capabilities

This section indicates UE capabilities for licensed-assisted access.

LAA capabilities
Downlink LAA

Support of DL LAA operation.

Remote command: 

SENSe:​LTE:​SIGN<i>:​UECapability:​LAA:​DL?

 
ending Dw PTS

Support of partial allocation in the ending subframe of an LAA burst.

Remote command: 

SENSe:​LTE:​SIGN<i>:​UECapability:​LAA:​EDPTs?

 
second Slot Starting Position

Support of partial allocation in the initial subframe of an LAA burst.

Remote command: 

SENSe:​LTE:​SIGN<i>:​UECapability:​LAA:​SSSPosition?

 
tm9-LAA

Support of transmission mode 9 for LAA downlinks.

Remote command: 

SENSe:​LTE:​SIGN<i>:​UECapability:​LAA:​TM<TMnr>?
