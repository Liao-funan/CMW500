PREPare:LTE:SIGN<i>:HANDover:EXTernal:WCDMa <Band>, <DLChannel>

Configures the destination parameters for handover to a WCDMA destination at another instrument.

Parameters:
<Band>
OB1 | OB2 | OB3 | OB4 | OB5 | OB6 | OB7 | OB8 | OB9 | OB10 | OB11 | OB12 | OB13 | OB14 | OB19 | OB20 | OB21 | OB22 | OB25 | OBS1 | OBS2 | OBS3 | OBL1 | OB26

OB1, ..., OB14: band I to XIV

OB19, ..., OB22: band XIX to XXII

OB25, OB26: band XXV, XXVI

OBS1: band S

OBS2: band S 170 MHz

OBS3: band S 190 MHz

OBL1: band L

*RST:OB1
<DLChannel>

Downlink channel number

Range: Depends on operating band, see table below
Example: 

See "Performing a Handover to Another Instrument"

Firmware/Software: 

V3.0.20, V3.5.40: OB22/OB25, V3.7.10: OB26

Manual operation: See "Destination Parameters"
