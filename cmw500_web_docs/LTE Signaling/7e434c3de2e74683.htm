CONFigure:LTE:SIGN<i>:UEReport:RINTerval <Interval>

Sets the interval between two consecutive measurement reports.

Parameters:
<Interval>
I120 | I240 | I480 | I640 | I1024 | I2048 | I5120 | I10240

Interval in ms, e.g. I240 = 240 ms

*RST:I1024
