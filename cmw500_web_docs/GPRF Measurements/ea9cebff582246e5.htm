CONFigure:GPRF:MEAS<i>:FFTSanalyzer:FSPan <FrequencySpan>

Selects the calculated and displayed frequency range (span) of the FFT spectrum analyzer.

Parameters:
<FrequencySpan>
Range: 1.25 MHz | 2.5 MHz | 5 MHz | 10 MHz | 20 MHz | 40 MHz | 80 MHz | 160 MHz
*RST:10 MHz
Default unit: Hz
Example: 

See "Specifying Measurement-Specific Settings"

Firmware/Software: 

V3.0.10, V3.7.10 added 80 MHz and 160 MHz

Options: R&S CMW with TRX160 required for 80 MHz and 160 MHz
Manual operation: See "Frequency Span"
