Measurement Results

Power results are either presented as summary statistics (per power step) or as statistical distributions or densities (only for single-step measurements). In list mode, all power results of the sweep can be displayed in a bar graph or in tables.

The summary statistics include the standard deviation that indicates how widely spread the power values are at each power step.
