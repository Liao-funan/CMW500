CONFigure:GPRF:MEAS<i>:IQRecorder:FILTer:TYPE <FilterType>

Selects the IF filter type. Gauss filters have shorter settling times and therefore larger sampling rates. Bandpass filters have a flat passband and steeper edges. See also "Filter Settings and Samples".

Parameters:
<FilterType>
BANDpass | GAUSs

BANDpass: bandpass filter with variable bandwidth

GAUSs: filter of Gaussian shape with variable bandwidth

*RST:GAUS
