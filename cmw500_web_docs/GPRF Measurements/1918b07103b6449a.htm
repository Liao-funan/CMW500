Measurement Results

The basic I/Q vs. slot measurement results are the I and Q amplitudes of the measured RF signal, averaged over the specified "Measurement Length", assuming a constant RF frequency. It is possible to convert the I/Q amplitudes into a pair of level and phase values. The R&S CMW also displays the frequency error for each measurement step.
