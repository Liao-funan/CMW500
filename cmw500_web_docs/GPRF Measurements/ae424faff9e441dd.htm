CONFigure:GPRF:MEAS<i>:POWer:LIST:TXITiming <Timing>

Specifies the timing of the generated "GPRF Meas<i>:Power" trigger.

Parameters:
<Timing>
STEP | CENTered

STEP: Trigger signals are generated at the step boundaries.

CENTered: Trigger signals are generated in the middle of the gaps between subsequent measurement intervals.

*RST:STEP
Firmware/Software: 

V3.0.11

Manual operation: See "TX Increment Timing"
