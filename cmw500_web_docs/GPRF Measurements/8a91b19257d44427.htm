I/Q vs. Slot Measurement

The "I/Q vs. Slot" tab provides all measurement results and displays the most important measurement settings.

To configure measurement settings, press the hotkey "Config". It opens a dialog box containing several tabs with settings. The tabs are described in the following.

The I/Q vs. slot measurement requires option CMW-KM011.
