Power Measurement

The power measurement measures a series of measurement steps at (possibly) different power and frequency levels and performs a statistical evaluation. The measurement is a flexible tool for power measurements on an arbitrary RF. In particular, it can be used for fast transmitter adjustments over a wide range of channels and output powers.
