CONFigure:GPRF:MEAS<i>:IQRecorder:FILTer:GAUSs:BWIDth <GaussBW>

Selects the bandwidth for a filter of Gaussian shape.

Parameters:
<GaussBW>
Range: 1 kHz | 10 kHz | 100 kHz | 1 MHz | 10 MHz (values in-between are rounded)
*RST:100 kHz
Default unit: Hz
