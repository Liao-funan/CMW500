Filter Settings and Samples

The R&S CMW provides a wide selection of Gaussian and bandpass filters to record the I/Q data. Gaussian filters have shorter settling times and therefore larger sampling rates. Bandpass filters have a flat passband and steeper edges.

The filter settings determine the maximum sampling rate as shown below. It is possible to reduce the maximum sampling rate by a factor and thus increase the measurement time, see CONFigure:​GPRF:​MEAS<i>:​IQRecorder:​RATio.
