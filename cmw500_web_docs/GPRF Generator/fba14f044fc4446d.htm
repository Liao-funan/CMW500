Constant-Frequency Signals

In constant-frequency mode, the RF generator provides a single-tone or dual-tone RF signal at constant frequency and output level.

  • The single-tone (CW) signal is configured with its level and frequency. No further signal settings are needed.
  • The two sources of the dual-tone signal can be configured separately, see below.

The dual-tone signal is a superposition of two CW signals "Source 1" and "Source 2" at the output connector. The R&S CMW provides two independent parameter sets for the two signals. The level of the signals is set implicitly via the sum level "Output Level (RMS)" and the "Ratio".
