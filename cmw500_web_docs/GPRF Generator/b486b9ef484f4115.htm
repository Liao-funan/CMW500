General Description

The R&S CMW provides a modulated or unmodulated RF carrier signal. The RF carrier signal and the modulating (baseband) signal can be configured independently. The RF carrier settings provide the following GPRF generator modes:

  • Constant-frequency mode: The RF generator provides an RF carrier signal at constant frequency. See also "Constant-Frequency Signals".
  • List mode: The RF carrier steps through a list of configurable frequencies and levels. See also "List Mode".
The baseband settings provide the following signal types:
  • CW: A CW signal is an unmodulated signal at the frequency and level of the RF carrier signal.
  • Dual tone: A dual tone signal is a superposition of two CW signals.
  • ARB: An ARB signal is based on an arbitrary baseband signal defined by a waveform file (*.wv, ARB file).
