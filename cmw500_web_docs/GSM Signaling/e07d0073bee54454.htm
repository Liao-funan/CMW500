SOURce:GSM:SIGN<i>:CELL:STATe <Control>

Turns the GSM signaling generator (DL GSM signal) off or on.

Setting parameters: 
<Control>
ON | OFF

Switch generator ON or OFF

*RST:OFF
Return values: 
<GeneratorState>
OFF | PENDing | ON | RFHandover

OFF: generator switched off

PEND: generator switched on but no signal available yet

ON: generator switched on, signal available

RFHandover: ready to receive a handover from another signaling application

*RST:OFF
