CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:FADing:FSIMulator:STANdard <Standard>

Selects one of the multipath propagation condition profiles defined in annex C.3 of 3GPP TS 45.005.

Parameters:
<Standard>
TI5 | T1P5 | T3 | T3P6 | T6 | T50 | T60 | T100 | H100 | H120 | H200 | R130 | R250 | R300 | R500 | E50 | E60 | E100 | T25 | TU1P5 | TU3 | TU25 | TU50 | HT100

The letter indicates the type of the model as follows:

TI: TI (2 path)

T: TUx (6 path)

H: HTx (6 path)

R: RAx (6 path)

E: EQx (6 path)

TU: TUx (12 path)

HT: HTx (12 path)

The number indicates the speed of the mobile in km/h.

Example: HT100 means 100 km/h, T1P5 means 1.5 km/h.

*RST:T3
