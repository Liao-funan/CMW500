PREPare:GSM:SIGN<i>:HANDover:EXTernal:GSM <Band>, <DLChannel>, <BandIndicator>

Configures the destination parameters for handover to a GSM destination at another instrument.

For channel number ranges depending on operating bands see "GSM Bands and Channels".

Parameters:
<Band>
G085 | G09 | G18 | G19

GSM 850, GSM 900, GSM 1800, GSM 1900

*RST:G09
<DLChannel>

Channel number used for the broadcast control channel (BCCH)

Range: 0 to 1023, depending on GSM band
*RST:20
<BandIndicator>
G18 | G19

Band indicator for distinction of GSM 1800 and GSM 1900 bands. The two bands partially use the same channel numbers for different frequencies.

*RST:G18
Example: 

See "Performing a CS Handover to Another Instrument"
