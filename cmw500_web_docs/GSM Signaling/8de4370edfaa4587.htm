CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:CONNection:RFOFfset <RandomFrqOffset>

Enables random frequency offset for the traffic channel. The R&S CMW randomly applies the positive and negative frequency offset.

Parameters:
<RandomFrqOffset>
OFF | ON
*RST:OFF
