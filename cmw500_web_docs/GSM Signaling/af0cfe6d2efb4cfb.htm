Limit Settings

The "Limits" section of the "GSM BER Configuration" dialog defines upper limits for the results of the BER PS measurement.

The conformance test specification 3GPP TS 51.010 specifies various test cases related to the BER limits. Adjust the values in accordance with your measurement.

Limit settings
Limits

Defines individual upper limits for the results of the "BER PS" measurement.

The "Class II Bits" limit applies to the "BER" result.
