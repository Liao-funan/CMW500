CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:CONNection:FOFFset:DL <Offset>
CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:CONNection:FOFFset[:UL] <Offset>

Sets the positive or negative offset to the center frequency of the uplink/downlink traffic channel.

Parameters:
<Offset>
Range: -100E+3 Hz  to  100E+3 Hz
*RST:0 Hz
Default unit: Hz
