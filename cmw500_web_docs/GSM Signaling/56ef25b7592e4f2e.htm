CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:CONNection:CSWitched:VAMos:MSLevel <Mode>

Selects the VAMOS support level of the mobile.

Parameters:
<Mode>
AUTO | VAM1 | VAM2

AUTO: according to the reported MS capabilities

VAM1: VAMOS support level I

VAM2: VAMOS support level II

*RST:VAM1

See "Configuring VAMOS Support"
