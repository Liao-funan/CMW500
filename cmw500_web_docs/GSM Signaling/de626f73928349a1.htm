CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:CELL:CDEScription <Number>(64)

Specifies the allowed DL traffic channels within the simulated GSM cell.

Parameters:
<Number>
ON | OFF

64 entries: one or several channel numbers in parallel, ON (OFF) switches on (off) a channel.

Range: 0 Ch  to  1023 Ch
