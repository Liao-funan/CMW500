CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:UL:MUEPower <MaxUEpower>

Sets the maximum allowed output power of the UE transmitter (averaged over the transmit slot).

Parameters:
<MaxUEpower>
Range: -50 dBm  to  33 dBm
*RST:33 dBm
Default unit: dBm
