CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CONNection:VOICe:AMR:WIDE <Rate>

Selects the mode of the WB AMR codec. The basic modes support one fixed bit-rate. Mode M supports several bit-rates.

Parameters:
<Rate>
A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H | I | M | M1 | M2

A: 23.85 kbps

B: 23.05 kbps

C: 19.85 kbps

D: 18.25 kbps

E: 15.85 kbps

F: 14.25 kbps

G: 12.65 kbps

H: 8.85 kbps

I: 6.60 kbps

M: G + H + I

M1: E + G + H + I

M2: A + G + H + I

*RST:I

V1.0.15.0

V3.5.40: added M1, M2
