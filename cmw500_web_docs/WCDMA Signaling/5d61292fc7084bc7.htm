CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:ERGCh:ETFCi:INITial <Index>

Position of the initial operating point in the expected E-TFCI table. If the operating point of the UE is shifted outside the E-TFCI range, the initial operating point is readjusted.

See also: CONFigure:​WCDMa:​SIGN<i>:​ERGCh:​ETFCi:​MANual and

CONFigure:​WCDMa:​SIGN<i>:​ERGCh:​ETFCi:​AUTO?

Parameters:
<Index>
Range: 2  to  (No. of expected ETFCI) - 1
*RST:5
