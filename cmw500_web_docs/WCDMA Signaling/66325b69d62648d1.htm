CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CONNection:TMODe:BTFD:TFORmat <DataRate>

Selects the downlink bit rate for BTFD RMCs.

Parameters:
<DataRate>
R1K95 | R4K75 | R5K15 | R5K9 | R6K7 | R7K4 | R7K95 | R10K2 | R12K2

Data rate in kbit/s: 1.95, 4.75, 5.15, 5.9, 6.7, 7.4, 7.95, 10.2, 12.2

*RST:R12K2
