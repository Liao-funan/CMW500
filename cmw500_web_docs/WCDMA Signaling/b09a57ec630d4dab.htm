RLC UE Capabilities

The UE capability information in the RLC section indicates in which way the UE supports the radio link control acknowledged mode (RLC AM).

RLC UE capabilities
RLC
  • "AM Buffer Size":
    Maximum total buffer size across all RLC AM entities supported by the UE
  • "Max. RLC Window Size":
    Maximum RLC window size supported by the UE
  • "Max. AM Entities":
    Maximum number of AM entities supported by the UE
  • "Two Logical Channels Config":
    Support of AM entity configured with two logical channels
