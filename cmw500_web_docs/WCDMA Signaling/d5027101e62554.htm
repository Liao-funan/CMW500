CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CELL:HSDPa:CQI:FOLLow <MinValue>, <MaxValue>

Defines the allowed range of CQI table indices. A value proposed by the UE is accepted if it is located within the range and FOLLow is configured via CONFigure:​WCDMa:​SIGN<i>:​CELL:​HSDPa:​CQI:​TINDex.

Parameters:
<MinValue>
Range: 1  to  30
*RST:1
<MaxValue>
Range: 1  to  30
*RST:30
