CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CMODe:UEReport:ENABle <FDD>, <GSMRSSI>, <GSMBSIC>, <GSMBSICReconf>, <EUTRA>

Enables the transmission gap patterns for different measurement purposes. All selected patterns are used in parallel.

Parameters:
<FDD>
OFF | ON
*RST:OFF
<GSMRSSI>
OFF | ON
*RST:OFF
<GSMBSIC>
OFF | ON
*RST:OFF
<GSMBSICReconf>
OFF | ON
*RST:OFF
<EUTRA>
OFF | ON
*RST:OFF
