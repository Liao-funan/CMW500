CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:UL:GFACtor:VOICe <BetaC>, <BetaD>

Specifies the UE gain factors βc (DPCCH) and βd (DPDCH) for voice connections.

Parameters:
<BetaC>
Range: 1  to  15
*RST:11
<BetaD>
Range: 1  to  15
*RST:15
