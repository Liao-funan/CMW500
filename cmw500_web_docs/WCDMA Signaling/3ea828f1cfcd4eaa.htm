CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:FADing:CARRier<c>:FSIMulator:STANdard <Standard>

Selects one of the propagation conditions defined in the annex B.2 of 3GPP TS 25.101.

Suffix: 
<c>
1..*

Downlink carrier

Parameters:
<Standard>
C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | C6 | C8 | PA3 | PB3 | VA3 | VA30 | VA12 | MPRopagation | BDEath | HST

C1 to C6: case 1 to case 6 (multipath fading profile)

C8: case 8 (for CQI test in multipath fading and HS-SCCH-less demodulation of HS-DSCH)

PA3 | PB3: ITU PA3 / PB3 (multipath fading profile)

VA3 | VA30 | VA12: ITU VA3 / VA30 / VA120 (multipath fading profile)

MPRopagation: moving propagation

BDEath: birth-death propagation

HST: high-speed train

*RST:C1
Example: 

See "Configuring Internal Fading"
