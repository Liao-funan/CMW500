CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:UL:GFACtor:HSDPa <BetaC>, <BetaD>, <DeltaACK>, <DeltaNACK>, <DeltaCQI>

Specifies the UE gain factors and power offsets for HSDPA connections.

Parameters:
<BetaC>
Range: 1  to  15
*RST:9
<BetaD>
Range: 1  to  15
*RST:15
<DeltaACK>
Range: 0  to  8
*RST:5
<DeltaNACK>
Range: 0  to  8
*RST:5
<DeltaCQI>
Range: 0  to  8
*RST:2
Example: 

See "Configuring Physical Channel UL Settings"
