CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CONNection:SRBData <Downlink>, <Uplink>

Selects the SRB data rate for downlink and uplink.

Parameters:
<Downlink>
R1K7 | R2K5 | R3K4 | R13K6

In kbit/s: 1.7, 2.5, 3.4, 13.6

*RST:R13K6
<Uplink>
R1K7 | R2K5 | R3K4 | R13K6

In kbit/s: 1.7, 2.5, 3.4, 13.6

*RST:R13K6
Example: 

See "Configuring Connection Types"

Firmware/Software: 

V1.0.15.0

Manual operation: See "SRB Data Rate"
