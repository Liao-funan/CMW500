CALL:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:RSIGnaling:ACTion <RSAction>

Switches the reduced signaling connection on or off, i.e. activates or deactivates the dedicated (and shared) downlink channels.

As a prerequisite for switching on the connection, the cell signal has to be switched on, see SOURce:​WCDMa:​SIGN<i>:​CELL:​STATe.

Setting parameters: 
<RSAction>
ON | OFF

ON: Switch on the reduced signaling connection

OFF: Switch off the reduced signaling connection
