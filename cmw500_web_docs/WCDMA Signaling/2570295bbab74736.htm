CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:DL:CARRier<c>:ENHanced:HSSCch:USFRames <Type>

Defines the transmission in unscheduled HS-SCCH subframes.

Suffix: 
<c>
1..*

Downlink carrier

Parameters:
<Type>
DUMMy | DTX

DUMMy: maintain HS-SCCH power and transfer dummy UE ID, see CONFigure:​WCDMa:​SIGN<i>:​DL:​CARRier<c>:​HSSCch<no>:​IDDummy

DTX: switch off output power in unscheduled subframes

*RST:DUMM
