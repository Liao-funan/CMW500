CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CELL:HSUPa:MCCode <Code>

Specifies the maximum channelization codes value signaled to the UE. Depending on several other HSUPA parameters, e.g. the UE category, only a subset of values is allowed.

Parameters:
<Code>
S64 | S32 | S16 | S8 | S4 | S24 | S22 | S224

S64, S32, S16, S8, S4: one code, SF 64 to SF 4

S24: two codes, SF 4

S22: two codes, SF 2

S224: four codes, two with SF 2 and two with SF 4

*RST:S224

See "Configuring HSUPA Settings"
