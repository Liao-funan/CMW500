SOURce:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CELL:STATe <Control>

Turns the generator (the cell) on or off.

See also: "Generator Control"

Setting parameters: 
<Control>
ON | OFF

Switch generator ON or OFF

*RST:OFF
Return values: 
<GeneratorState>
OFF | PENDing | ON

OFF: generator switched off

PEND: generator switched on but no signal available yet

ON: generator switched on, signal available

*RST:OFF
