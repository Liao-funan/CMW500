CONFigure:LTE:MEAS<i>:ENB:MEValuation:CBANdwidth <ChannelBW>

Selects the channel bandwidth of the measured LTE signal.

Parameters:
<ChannelBW>
B014 | B030 | B050 | B100 | B150 | B200

B014: 1.4 MHz

B030: 3 MHz

B050: 5 MHz

B100: 10 MHz

B150: 15 MHz

B200: 20 MHz

*RST:B200
