How to Measure a Downlink LTE Signal
After connecting your eNodeB to the R&S CMW, you have to adjust at least the following analyzer settings to the properties of the analyzed DL LTE signal:
  • Duplex mode
  • Analyzer "Frequency"
  • "Expected Nominal Power", "User Margin" and "External Attenuation (Input)"
    Recommended values: "Expected Nominal Power" = peak power of the signal during the measurement; "User Margin" = 0 dB. The smallest possible value of the "Expected Nominal Power" plus the "User Margin" ensures maximum dynamic range.
For synchronization to the received signal and proper decoding, several "Measurement Control" settings in the configuration dialog must be in accordance with the measured signal. In particular, ensure that the following parameters match up:
  • "E-UTRA test model"
  • "Channel Bandwidth"
  • "Physical Cell ID"
  • "MIMO Configuration" and "TX Antenna Selection"
  • For TDD: "Uplink Downlink" configuration and "Special Subframe" configuration

With matching "Measurement Control" settings, the R&S CMW is able to decode the signal and to determine its frame, subframe and slot timing. No additional measurement trigger is required (see "Trigger Modes").

Non-matching "Measurement Control" settings generally result in large EVM results or cause a synchronization error.
