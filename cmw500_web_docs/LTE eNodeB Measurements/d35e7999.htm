CONFigure:LTE:MEAS<i>:ENB:MEValuation:MODulation:EWLength <CPnormB014>, <CPnormB030>, <CPnormB050>, <CPnormB100>, <CPnormB150>, <CPnormB200>

Specifies the EVM window length in samples for all channel bandwidths.

Parameters:
<CPnormB014>

Length for normal CP, BW = 1.4 MHz

Range: 1  to  9
*RST:5
<CPnormB030>

Length for normal CP, BW = 3 MHz

Range: 1  to  18
*RST:12
<CPnormB050>

Length for normal CP, BW = 5 MHz

Range: 1  to  36
*RST:32
<CPnormB100>

Length for normal CP, BW = 10 MHz

Range: 1  to  72
*RST:66
<CPnormB150>

Length for normal CP, BW = 15 MHz

Range: 1  to  108
*RST:102
<CPnormB200>

Length for normal CP, BW = 20 MHz

Range: 1  to  144
*RST:136
