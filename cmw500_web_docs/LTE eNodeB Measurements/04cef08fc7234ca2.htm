CONFigure:LTE:MEAS<i>:ENB:MEValuation:RESult:OOPower <Enable>

Enables or disables the evaluation of the ON/OFF power results and shows or hides the view in the multi-evaluation measurement.

The view can only be enabled for TDD signals.

Parameters:
<Enable>
OFF | ON

OFF: Do not evaluate results, hide the view

ON: Evaluate results and show the view

*RST:OFF
