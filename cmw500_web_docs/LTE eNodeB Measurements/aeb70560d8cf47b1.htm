CONFigure:LTE:MEAS<i>:ENB:MEValuation:TASelection:INPut<In> <TXantennaSelect>

Specifies the numbers of the antennas connected to the two RF inputs of the "Two RF In" scenario. The first RF input is also used for the "Standalone" scenario.

Suffix: 
<In>
1..2

RF input 1 or 2

Parameters:
<TXantennaSelect>
A1 | A2

A1: Antenna 1 (SISO antenna or first MIMO 2x2 TX antenna)

A2: Antenna 2 (second MIMO 2x2 TX antenna)

*RST:A1
