Defining the Scope of the Measurement

Standard 3GPP TS 36.141 requests a measurement duration of 10 consecutive downlink subframes for all tests, except ON/OFF power tests.

Behavior for all tests, except ON/OFF power tests

All 10 subframes of a radio frame are evaluated for FDD downlink signals. For TDD signals, the first 10 downlink subframes of two consecutive radio frames are evaluated (with uplink subframes and special subframes in-between).

The statistic count (i.e. the length of a single shot) is configurable. By default, it equals 10 subframes. If you configure a different statistic count, 10 consecutive downlink subframes are captured repeatedly, until the statistic count is reached. Between these repetitions, there can be not-captured radio frames. Up to 1000 subframes can be captured per single shot.

Examples:
  • Statistic count = 5 subframes: 10 consecutive downlink subframes are captured. Results are provided for the first 5 subframes.
  • Statistic count = 17 subframes: 10 consecutive downlink subframes are captured and results are provided. The subsequent radio frames are ignored. Then 10 consecutive downlink subframes are captured. Results are provided for the first 7 subframes.
Behavior for ON/OFF power tests (TDD only)

The first 5 subframes of a radio frame are evaluated. The downlink power results are measured on subframe number 0. The OFF power results are measured on the uplink parts of the other subframes.

The statistic count is configurable as number of radio frames.

MIMO antenna selection

To test a MIMO 2x2 signal with the "Two RF In" scenario, you connect both antennas and measure one antenna at a time. Select the measured antenna via the "Measure Input" setting. Simply change this setting to switch between the two antennas. The other RF input is called "Additional Input" and is used for time alignment measurements.
