Occupied Bandwidth Limit

The occupied bandwidth is the width of a symmetric frequency interval around the nominal RF carrier frequency that contains 99 % of the total mean power of the transmitted spectrum.

According to 3GPP the NodeB transmitts at maximum output power. Verify the occupied bandwidth with the test model 1.
