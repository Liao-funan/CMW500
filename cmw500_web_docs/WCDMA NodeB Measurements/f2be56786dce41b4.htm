How to Measure a Downlink WCDMA Signal
After connecting your NodeB to the R&S CMW, you have to adjust at least the following analyzer settings to the properties of the analyzed DL WCDMA signal:
  • The analyzer band and frequency
  • The "Expected Nominal Power" and (optional) a "User Margin" and "External Attenuation (Input)".
    Recommended values: "Expected Nominal Power" = peak power of the NodeB signal over the entire measurement range; "User Margin" = 0 dB (the smallest possible value ensures maximum dynamic range).
For synchronization to the received signal and proper decoding, several measurement control settings in the configuration dialog must be in accordance with the measured signal. In particular, ensure that the following parameters match up:
  • The"Test Model"
  • The "Scrambling Code"

The R&S CMW can auto-detect the spreading factor of the DPDCH and the corresponding symbol rate.

With matching measurement control settings, the R&S CMW is able to decode the WCDMA DL signal and determine its slot timing. No additional measurement trigger is required.

Non-matching measurement control settings generally result in large EVM results or cause a synchronization error.
