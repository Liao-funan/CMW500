CONFigure:WLAN:SIGN<i>:PER:DFRame:HEMU:DUMMy<index>:MCS <MCS_Index>

Sets the modulation and coding scheme for the corresponding dummy user.

Suffix: 
<index>
1..3

Dummy user selection

Parameters:
<MCS_Index>
MCS | MCS1 | MCS2 | MCS3 | MCS4 | MCS5 | MCS6 | MCS7 | MCS8 | MCS9 | MCS10 | MCS11

MCS, MCS1,...,MCS11: MCS 0 to MCS 11

*RST:MCS
