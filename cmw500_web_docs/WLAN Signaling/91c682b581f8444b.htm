CONFigure:WLAN:SIGN<i>:CONNection:HETF:MCS <MCS>

Specifies the modulation and coding scheme (MCS) used by the HE TB PPDU.

Parameters:
<MCS>
MCS | MCS1 | MCS2 | MCS3 | MCS4 | MCS5 | MCS6 | MCS7 | MCS8 | MCS9 | MCS10 | MCS11

MCS, MCS1,...,MCS11: MCS 0 to MCS 11

*RST:MCS
