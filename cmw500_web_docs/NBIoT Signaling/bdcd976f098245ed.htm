BLER Measurement

The BLER measurement is installed together with the NB-IoT signaling application. To access the measurement, press the softkey "NB-IoT RX Meas" in the NB-IoT signaling main view.

The measurement sends data to the UE via the NPDSCH. It evaluates the positive acknowledgments (ACK) and negative acknowledgments (NACK) returned by the UE to determine the block error ratio (BLER) and the throughput.

The measurement is especially suitable to assess the characteristics and the performance of the UE receiver at low RF power levels.
