CONFigure:NIOT:SIGN<i>:CONNection:RRC:KEEP <Enable>

Selects whether the RRC connection is kept or released after attach.

Parameters:
<Enable>
OFF | ON

OFF: The RRC connection is released.

ON: The RRC connection is kept.

*RST:ON
