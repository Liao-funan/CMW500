Low Energy Results

The R&S CMW provides measurement diagrams and statistical results of Tx measurements (tab "Multi Evaluation") and statistical results of Rx measurements (tab "Rx Meas").

Option R&S CMW-KM611 is required for LE 1M PHY (uncoded). For LE 2M PHY and LE coded PHY, option R&S CMW-KM721 is also required.

Rx measurements require additional option R&S CMW-KD611 (only for R&S CMW100/CMW with MUA).
