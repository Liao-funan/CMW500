CONFigure:BLUetooth:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:LENergy[:LE1M]:PVTime <AvgPowLower>, <AvgPowUpper>, <PkmAvgPowUpper>, <AvgPowEnabled>(4), <PkmAvgPowEnable>(4)

Defines the power limits: lower and upper average power limits, upper limit for "peak minus average power", limit check enabling.

Commands for LE 1M PHY - uncoded (...:LE1M...), LE 2M PHY - uncoded (...:LE2M...), and LE coded PHY (...:LRANge...) are available.

Parameters:
<AvgPowLower>
Range: -99.99 dBm  to  99.99 dBm
*RST:-20 dBm
<AvgPowUpper>
Range: -99.99 dBm  to  99.99 dBm
*RST:10 dBm
<PkmAvgPowUpper>
Range: -99.99 dBm  to  99.99 dBm
*RST:3 dBm
<AvgPowEnabled>
OFF | ON

Disables or enables the limit check for the average power, 4 values, corresponding to the current, average, maximum and minimum results.

*RST:ON
<PkmAvgPowEnable>
OFF | ON

Disables or enables the limit check for the "peak minus average power", 4 values, corresponding to the current, average, maximum and minimum results.

*RST:ON
