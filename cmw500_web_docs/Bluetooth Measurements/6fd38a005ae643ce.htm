Defining the Scope of the Measurement

The Bluetooth multi-evaluation measurement extends over a single Bluetooth packet plus an appropriate margin before and after the edges. The packet length is defined by the payload length parameter. No other settings concerning the measurement duration are required.

If the measurement is performed repeatedly, the R&S CMW can evaluate the trace statistics. The number and position of the trace points is calculated as described in the following sections.
