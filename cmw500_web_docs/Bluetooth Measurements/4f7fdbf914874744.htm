Rx Measurement Results

The Rx measurement results are displayed in an "Rx Meas" tab.

Non-signaling Rx measurements are only supported by R&S CMW100/CMW with MUA.

The most important settings of the Bluetooth Rx measurement are displayed at the top of the measurement dialog. The settings display frequency, corresponding advertiser index, reference level, and the Bluetooth device address and the address type of the R&S CMW.

The tab shows the measurement results in a table. The type of displayed results depends on the selected measurement mode.

The "Config" hotkey opens either the configuration dialog of the Rx measurement or the configuration dialog of the Bluetooth multi-evaluation measurement application, depending on which softkey is active.
