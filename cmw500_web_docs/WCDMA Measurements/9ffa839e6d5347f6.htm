CONFigure:WCDMa:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:RESult:CHIP:MERRor <EnableMErrChip>

Enables or disables the evaluation of results and shows or hides the magnitude error vs. chip view in the multi-evaluation measurement.

Parameters:
<EnableMErrChip>
OFF | ON

OFF: Do not evaluate results, hide the view

ON: Evaluate results and show the view

*RST:OFF
Firmware/Software: 

V1.0.4.11

Manual operation: See "Assign Views (Hotkey)"
