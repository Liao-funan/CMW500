CONFigure:WCDMa:MEAS<i>:PRACh:RESult:MERRor <EnableMagError>

Enables or disables the evaluation of results and shows or hides the magnitude error view in the PRACH measurement.

Parameters:
<EnableMagError>
OFF | ON

OFF: Do not evaluate results, hide the view

ON: Evaluate results and show the view

*RST:ON
Firmware/Software: 

V3.0.20

Manual operation: See "Assign Views (Hotkey)"
