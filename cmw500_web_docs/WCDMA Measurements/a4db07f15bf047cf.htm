CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:UL:GFACtor:RMC<no> <BetaC>, <BetaD>

Specifies the UE gain factors βc (DPCCH) and βd (DPDCH) for RMC connections with the selected data rate.

Suffix: 
<no>
1..5

Selects the RMC data rate

1: 12.2 kbps

2: 64 kbps

3: 144 kbps

4: 384 kbps

5: 768 kbps

Parameters:
<BetaC>
Range: 1  to  15
*RST:8, 5, 4, 4, 4 for <no> 1 to 5
<BetaD>
Range: 1  to  15
*RST:15
Firmware/Software: 

V1.0.15.0

V2.1.30: added <no> = 5

V3.0.10: R&S CMW-KS410 no longer required

Manual operation: See "UE Channels"
