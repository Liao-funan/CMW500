TRIGger:WCDMa:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:SLOPe <Slope>

Qualifies whether the trigger event is generated at the rising or at the falling edge of the trigger pulse (valid for external and power trigger sources).

Parameters:
<Slope>
REDGe | FEDGe

REDGe: Rising edge

FEDGe: Falling edge

*RST:REDG
