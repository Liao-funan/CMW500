CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CONNection:TMODe:RMC:DRATe <Downlink>, <Uplink>

Selects the information bit rate of the downlink and uplink reference channel.

Parameters:
<Downlink>
R12K2 | R64K | R144k | R384k

R12K2: 12.2 kbps

R64K: 64 kbps

R144k: 144 kbps

R384k: 384 kbps

*RST:R12K2
<Uplink>
R12K2 | R64K | R144k | R384k | R768k

R12K2: 12.2 kbps

R64K: 64 kbps

R144k: 144 kbps

R384k: 384 kbps

R768k: 768 kbps

*RST:R12K2
Firmware/Software: 

V3.0.20

Options: R&S CMW-KS410
Manual operation: See "RMC > DL Resource in Use"
