CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:RFSettings:DBDC <Enable>[, <Config>]

Enables dual band dual carrier HSDPA operation and selects the operating bands for UL and DL.

Parameters:
<Enable>
OFF | ON
*RST:Off
<Config>
UDEFined | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | C6

UDEFined: User defined (custom) - free band selection

C1: DL band A I, DL band B VIII

C2: DL band A II, DL band B IV

C3: DL band A I, DL band B V

C4: DL band A I, DL band B XI

C5: DL band A II, DL band B V

C6: DL band A I, DL band B XXXII

UL applies the band of the DL carrier 1, where the assignment of band A or band B is possible. Exception: no UL for operating band XXXII.

*RST:C1
Firmware/Software: 

V3.2.60

Options: R&S CMW-KS405
Manual operation: See "Band / Channel / Frequency"
