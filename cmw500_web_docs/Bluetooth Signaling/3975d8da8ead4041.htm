CONFigure:BLUetooth:SIGN<i>:RXQuality:SEARch:RINTegrity:LENergy:LE2M <ReportIntegrity>
CONFigure:BLUetooth:SIGN<i>:RXQuality:SEARch:RINTegrity:LENergy[:LE1M] <ReportIntegrity>

Sets the ratio of the test packets with correct CRC transmitted by the R&S CMW.

Commands for LE 1M PHY (...:LE1M...), LE 2M PHY (...:LE2M...), and LE coded PHY (...:LRANge...) are available.

Parameters:
<ReportIntegrity>
OFF | ON

OFF: 100% of packets generated with correct CRC

ON: 50% of packets generated with correct CRC

*RST:OFF
