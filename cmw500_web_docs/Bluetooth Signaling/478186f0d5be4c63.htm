Operating Bands

The frequency band defined for Bluetooth devices is the unlicensed 2.4 GHz "Industrial, Scientific and Medical" (ISM) frequency band.

Operating band BR/EDR

Regulatory range

RF channels k

2400.0 MHz to 2483.5 MHz

k = 0 to 78, f = k + 2402 MHz

Operating band LE

Regulatory range

RF channels k

2400.0 MHz to 2483.5 MHz

k = 0 to 39, f = k*2 + 2402 MHz
