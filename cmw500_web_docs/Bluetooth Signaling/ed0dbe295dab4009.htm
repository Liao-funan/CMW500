CONFigure:BLUetooth:SIGN<i>:COMSettings<no>:BAUDrate <BaudRate>

Specifies the transmission parameters of serial connection.

Suffix: 
<no>
1..4

1: HW interface for LE tests

2: HW interface for BR / EDR tests

Parameters:
<BaudRate>
B110 | B300 | B600 | B12K | B24K | B48K | B96K | B14K | B19K | B28K | B38K | B57K | B115k | B234k | B460k | B500k | B576k | B921k | B1M | B1M5 | B2M | B3M | B3M5 | B4M

Data transmission rate in symbol: 110, 300, 600, 1200, 2400, 4800, 9600, 14400, 19200, 28800, 38400, 57600, 115200, 230400, 460800, 500000, 576000, 921600, 1000000, 1152000, 2000000, 3000000, 3500000, 4000000

*RST:B96K
