RX Quality Measurement

The "Bluetooth Signaling" RX quality measurement tests the performance on the complete signal path from the R&S CMW to the EUT and back. Operating mode RF test is required, see "Operating Mode, Profile".

The measurement is especially suitable to assess the characteristics and the performance of the EUT receiver at low RF power levels. The transmission errors of the higher signal level, produced on the way back (from the EUT to the instrument) can usually be neglected.

BER and PER Dialog

The R&S CMW provides a separate tab and configuration dialog for BER and PER measurements, to be accessed from the "Measurement Controller" dialog, entry "RX Quality".
