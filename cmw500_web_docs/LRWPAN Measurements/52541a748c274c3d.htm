CONFigure:WPAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:SCONdition <Stop Condition>

Qualifies whether the measurement is stopped after a failed limit check or continued. SLFail means that the measurement is stopped and reaches the RDY state as soon as one of the results exceeds the limits.

Parameters:
<Stop Condition>
NONE | SLFail

NONE: continue measurement irrespective of the limit check

SLFail: stop measurement on limit failure

*RST:NONE
