CONFigure:WPAN:MEAS<i>:DISPlay <Measurement>[, <View>]

Selects the view to be displayed.

Parameters:
<Measurement>
MEV

Multi-evaluation measurement

<View>
OVERview | PVTime | EVM | PSDensity | MODulation | POWer

OVERview: "Overview"

PVTime: "Power vs Time"

EVM: "Error Vector Magnitude"

PSDensity: "Power Spectral Density"

MODulation: "Modulation Scalars"

POWer: "Power Scalars"

*RST:OVER
