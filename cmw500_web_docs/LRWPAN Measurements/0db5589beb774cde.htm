CONFigure:WPAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:PVTime <AveragePower>, <AvgPowEnabled>

Sets and enables/disables a lower limit for PVT measurements.

Parameters:
<AveragePower>
Range: -50 dBm  to  55 dBm
*RST:-3 dBm
<AvgPowEnabled>
OFF | ON
*RST:ON
