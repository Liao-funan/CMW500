CONFigure:AUDio:WAV<i>:RECord:FILTer:HPASs <Frequency>

Configures the cutoff frequency of the highpass filter for waveform recording.

Parameters:
<Frequency>
OFF | H6 | H50

OFF: highpass filter disabled

H6 | H50: cutoff frequency 6 Hz or 50 Hz

*RST:H6
