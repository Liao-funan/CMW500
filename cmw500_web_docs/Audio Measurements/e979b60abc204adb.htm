INITiate:AUDio:WAV<i>:RECord
STOP:AUDio:WAV<i>:RECord
ABORt:AUDio:WAV<i>:RECord
Starts, stops, or aborts the recording:
  • INITiate... starts or continues the recording. The state switches to "RUN".
  • STOP... pauses the recording. The state switches to "RDY". The resources remain allocated.
  • ABORt... stops the recording and writes the waveform file. The state switches to "OFF". Allocated resources are released.
