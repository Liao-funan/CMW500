CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:ANALog:SCONdition <StopCondition>
CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:DIGital:SCONdition <StopCondition>

Specifies whether the measurement is stopped after a failed limit check or continued. OLFA means that the measurement is stopped and reaches the RDY state when one of the results exceeds the limits.

Parameters:
<StopCondition>
NONE | OLFA

NONE: Continue measurement irrespective of the limit check

OLFA: Stop measurement on limit failure

*RST:NONE
