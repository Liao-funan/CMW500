Single Tone Settings

The following settings are specific for single tone signals.

Single tone generator settings
└ Frequency
└ Level
Frequency

Specifies the frequency of the single tone to be generated.

Remote command: 

SOURce:​AUDio:​GEN<i>:​ANALog:​SINGletone:​FREQuency

SOURce:​AUDio:​GEN<i>:​DIGital:​SINGletone:​FREQuency

 
Level

Specifies the level of the single tone as effective voltage (RMS averaged) or as full-scale value.

Remote command: 

SOURce:​AUDio:​GEN<i>:​ANALog:​SINGletone:​LEVel

SOURce:​AUDio:​GEN<i>:​DIGital:​SINGletone:​LEVel
