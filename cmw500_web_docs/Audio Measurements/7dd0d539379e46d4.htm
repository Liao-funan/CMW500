CONFigure:AUDio:SPEech<i>:ANALog:FILTer:HPASs <Frequency>

Configures the cutoff frequency of the highpass filter, located between the AF IN connectors and the speech encoder.

Parameters:
<Frequency>
OFF | H6 | H50

OFF: highpass filter disabled

H6 | H50: cutoff frequency 6 Hz or 50 Hz

*RST:H6
Firmware/Software: 

V3.2.30

Manual operation: See "High Pass"
