Audio Measurement and Generator Scenario

This section provides examples for single tone, multitone and FFT noise measurements with the audio measurement and generator scenario.

The audio generators are programmed as follows:
  • The generators are controlled by SCPI commands with the following syntax:
    –Analog generator: ...AUDio:GEN:ANALog...
    –Digital generator: ...AUDio:GEN:DIGital...
  • After a *RST, the generator is switched off.
    To switch it on, use SOURce:WCDMa:GEN:STATe ON . *OPC? ensures that the generator signal is available before the next command line is executed.
The audio measurements are programmed as follows:
  • The measurements are controlled by SCPI commands with the following syntax:
    –Analog measurements: ...AUDio:MEAS:ANALog...
    –Digital measurements: ...AUDio:MEAS:DIGital...
  • After a *RST, the measurements are switched off.
    Use READ:AUDio:MEAS:ANALog/DIGital:...? to initiate a single-shot measurement and retrieve the results. You can also start the measurement using INIT:AUDio:MEAS:ANALog/DIGital and retrieve the results using FETCh:AUDio:MEAS:ANALog/DIGital:...?.
