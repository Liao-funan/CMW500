Test Setups for the Scenario Waveform

The scenario "Waveform" uses both audio software instances. You cannot use another scenario in parallel.

You can select which interfaces you want to use for playback and recording of a waveform file. The cabling depends on this selection.

Playback:
  • "Analog": The generated stereo signal is available at the two AF OUT connectors.
  • "Digital": The generated stereo signal is available at the SPDIF OUT connector.
  • "Speech": The generated stereo signal is routed to the internal speech encoder and thus to a signaling application.
Recording:
  • "Analog": The recorded stereo signal comes from the two AF IN connectors.
  • "Digital": The recorded stereo signal comes from the SPDIF IN connector.
  • "Speech": The recorded stereo signal comes from the internal speech decoder. The decoder is fed by a signaling application.

The selections for playback and recording are independent (except the controlling application for speech). You can play an audio file and in parallel record another audio file.

For "Speech", you need a signaling application and a speech codec.
