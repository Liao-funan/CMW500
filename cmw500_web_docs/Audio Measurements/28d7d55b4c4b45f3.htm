CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:ANALog:REPetition <Repetition>
CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:DIGital:REPetition <Repetition>

Specifies the repetition mode of the measurement. The repetition mode specifies whether the measurement is stopped after a single-shot or repeated continuously.

See also: "Statistical Settings"

Parameters:
<Repetition>
SINGleshot | CONTinuous

SINGleshot: Single-shot measurement

CONTinuous: Continuous measurement

*RST:SING
