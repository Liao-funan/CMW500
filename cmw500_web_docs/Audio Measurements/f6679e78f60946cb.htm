CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:ANALog:FILTer:WEIGhting <SingleTone>, <Multitone>, <FFTNoise>
CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:DIGital:FILTer:WEIGhting <SingleTone>, <Multitone>, <FFTNoise>

Configures the weighting filter for the individual audio signal types.

Parameters:
<SingleTone>
OFF | AWEight | CCITt | CMSG

OFF: weighting filter disabled

AWEight: A-weighting filter

CCITt: CCITT weighting filter

CMSG: C-message weighting filter

*RST:OFF
<Multitone>
OFF | AWEight | CCITt | CMSG
*RST:OFF
<FFTNoise>
OFF | AWEight | CCITt | CMSG
*RST:OFF
