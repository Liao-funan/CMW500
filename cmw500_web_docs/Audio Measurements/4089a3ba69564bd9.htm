CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:ANALog:SPECtrum:DMODe <DiagramMode>
CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:DIGital:SPECtrum:DMODe <DiagramMode>

Enables or disables the evaluation of the single tone spectrum results and shows or hides the spectrum diagram.

Parameters:
<DiagramMode>
OFF | ON

OFF: Do not evaluate the results and hide the diagram

ON: Evaluate the results and show the diagram

*RST:OFF
Example: 

See "Single Tone Measurement"

Firmware/Software: 

V3.2.20

Manual operation: See "Spectrum > Off / On"
