Audio Connectors

An R&S CMW with an installed audio board provides AF connectors for analog audio signals on the front panel and SPDIF connectors for digital audio signals on the rear panel.

AF connectors

Connector AF 1 IN and AF 2 IN are used for the input of analog audio signals. Connector AF 1 OUT and AF 2 OUT are used for the output of analog audio signals.

SPDIF connectors

Connector SPDIF IN is used for the input of digital audio signals, SPDIF OUT for the output of digital audio signals. A digital signal can contain two multiplexed channels, SPDIF-L and SPDIF-R.

The Sony/Philips Digital Interface (SPDIF) connectors are in line with IEC 60958-3:2010 Consumer.

Connector selection

Which audio connector is used, depends on the instance of the audio application and on the subinstrument configuration of the instrument, as shown in the following table.

Analog stereo test scenarios use AF 1 and AF 2. Digital stereo test scenarios use both SPDIF channels.

The waveform scenario works in stereo mode. So it also uses two channels/connectors. You can select between analog and digital connectors (or the internal interface to the speech codec).
