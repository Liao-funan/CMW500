Audio Measurements
The "Audio Analyzer/Generator" option enhances the R&S CMW for audio tests. The option (R&S CMW-B400B or R&S CMW-U400) provides:
  • One audio board
  • Two analog and two digital audio generator instances
  • Two analog and two digital audio measurement instances
  • Four analog AF connectors on the front panel
  • Two digital SPDIF connectors on the rear panel

You can use all generator and measurement instances in parallel. So you can generate and measure two analog mono audio test signals and two digital mono audio test signals in parallel.

The tests cover the following audio signal properties:
  • Characteristics of a single tone signal, for example total harmonic distortion (THD), signal to noise and distortion ratio (SINAD) and signal to noise ratio (SNR).
  • Audio levels of up to 20 single tones at configurable frequencies and levels, combined to a multitone signal.
  • Audio level vs. frequency for a noise signal, consisting of several 1000 single tones. The noise signal has a low crest factor. It is ideal to analyze the frequency response of a DUT over the entire audio frequency range, with a single measurement.
Two audio analyzer and generator options are available. They provide the same test functionality. Which option is required, depends on your instrument:
  • Option R&S CMW-B400B "Audio Analyzer/Generator" is compatible to R&S CMW delivered after September 2011. It is also compatible to R&S CMW delivered before September 2011, that have been upgraded with R&S CMW-U5024.
  • Option R&S CMW-U400 "Audio Analyzer/Generator" is compatible to R&S CMW delivered before September 2011, that have not been upgraded with R&S CMW-U5024.

Optional speech codec

The audio board can be equipped with none, one or two speech codecs. A single speech codec is available as option R&S CMW-B405A.

A speech codec provides an interface to the signaling units of the R&S CMW. It decodes the signal delivered by a controlling application or encodes an audio signal and delivers it to the controlling application.

Thus the speech codec allows you to feed an audio signal into an established RF connection to the DUT or to extract an audio signal from the RF connection. The controlling application can be a signaling application, the IMS server of the DAU (for VoLTE) or a protocol test application.

The audio signal can be generated and analyzed internally using the digital audio generator and measurement. This feature allows, for example, microphone- and speaker tests for a mobile phone.

For speech quality analysis of a mobile phone, an external audio analyzer is required (e.g. R&S UPV). It is connected to the audio connectors of the R&S CMW. The audio signal is generated and analyzed by the external audio analyzer.

A single speech codec provides a bidirectional interface (encoding and decoding) for a single controlling application instance at a time. If you have two speech codecs, you can use them in parallel with different audio software instances and different controlling application instances.
