CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:ANALog:SINGletone:FILTer:HPASs <Frequency>
CONFigure:AUDio:MEAS<i>:DIGital:SINGletone:FILTer:HPASs <Frequency>

Configures the cutoff frequency of the highpass filter for single tone measurements.

Parameters:
<Frequency>
OFF | H6 | H50 | H300

OFF: highpass filter disabled

H6 | H50 | H300: cutoff frequency 6 Hz, 50 Hz or 300 Hz

*RST:ANALog = OFF, DIGital = H6
Example: 

See "Single Tone Measurement"

Firmware/Software: 

V3.2.10

Manual operation: See "Filter > Low Pass, High Pass, Weighting, Variable Bandpass"
