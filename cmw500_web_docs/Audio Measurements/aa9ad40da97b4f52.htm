CONFigure:AUDio:WAV<i>:PLAYback:OLEVel <OutputLevel>

Configures the D/A conversion for the analog destination of waveform playback.

Parameters:
<OutputLevel>

Analog output signal peak voltage corresponding to digital signal full-scale value

Range: 0 V  to  7 V
*RST:0.3 V
Default unit: V
Example: 

See "Playing a Waveform File"

Firmware/Software: 

V3.7.20
