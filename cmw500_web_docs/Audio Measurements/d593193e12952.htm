SOURce:AUDio:GEN<i>:ANALog:SINGletone:LEVel <Level>

Specifies the level of the analog single tone signal as effective voltage (RMS averaged).

Parameters:
<Level>
Range: 0 V  to  5 V
*RST:1 V
Default unit: V
