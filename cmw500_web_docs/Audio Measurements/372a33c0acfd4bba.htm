SOURce:AUDio:GEN<i>:ANALog:MULTitone:CRESt <CrestFactor>
SOURce:AUDio:GEN<i>:DIGital:MULTitone:CRESt <CrestFactor>

Configures the crest factor of the generated multitone signal.

Parameters:
<CrestFactor>
MAX | LOW

MAX: maximum crest factor

LOW: low crest factor

*RST:MAX
