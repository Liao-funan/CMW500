CONFigure:AUDio<i>:CDRift <ClockDrift>

Adjusts the clock of the audio board.

Parameters:
<ClockDrift>
Range: -100 ppm  to  100 ppm
*RST:0 ppm
Default unit: ppm

Additional parameters: OFF | ON (disables | enables the clock adjustment)

Firmware/Software: 

V3.5.12

Manual operation: See "Clock Drift"
