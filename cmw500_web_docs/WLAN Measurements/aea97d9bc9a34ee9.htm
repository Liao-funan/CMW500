CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:ISIGnal:BWIDth <BandWidth>

Selects the channel bandwidth. In the combined signal path scenario, the bandwidth is fixed to 20 MHz.

Parameters:
<BandWidth>
BW05mhz | BW10mhz | BW20mhz | BW40mhz | BW80mhz | BW88mhz | BW16mhz

BW05mhz: 5 MHz (802.11p)

BW10mhz: 10 MHz (802.11p)

BW20mhz: 20 MHz (all standards)

BW40mhz: 40 MHz (802.11n, 802.11ac, 802.11ax)

BW80mhz: 80 MHz (802.11ac, 802.11ax)

BW88mhz: 80+80 MHz (802.11ac)

BW16mhz: 160 MHz (802.11ac, 802.11ax)

*RST:BW20
