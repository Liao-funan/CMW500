CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:COMPensation:TRACking:PHASe <Phase>

Activate or deactivate phase tracking. With enabled tracking, fluctuations are compensated.

For composite MIMO and 802.11ac input signals, phase tracking is always enabled.

Parameters:
<Phase>
OFF | ON

OFF: Tracking disabled

ON: Tracking enabled

*RST:ON
