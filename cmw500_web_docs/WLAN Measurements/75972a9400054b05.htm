V3.2.10 (Compared to V3.0.20)
New features:
  • IEEE 802.11ac SISO measurements (option R&S CMW-KM656):
    –Support for MCS 8 and 9 (256-QAM) signals
    –Up to 80 MHz bandwidth
    –Multiple simultaneous measurements: EVM vs. symbol, I/Q constellation, spectrum flatness, transmit spectrum mask, TX measurement (scalar)
    –Limit checks for transmitter constellation error, transmit center frequency error, symbol clock frequency error, spectrum flatness and transmit spectrum mask, see "Limit Settings"
  • "IQ Constellation" view for IEEE 11n SISO measurements
  • User-defined "Frequency Offset", see "Frequency Offset"
  • Modified mnemonics in remote commands:
    –NOTWenty replaced by NSTWenty in limit commands for 802.11n SISO signals with 20 MHz bandwidth
    –NOForty replaced by NSForty in limit commands for 802.11n SISO signals with 40 MHz bandwidth
    –NOFDm replaced by NSISo in remote commands for 802.11n SISO signals
    –MIMO replaced by SMIMo in commands that only apply to switched 802.11n MIMO measurements (modulation limits and related results, number of antennas)
  • Default "Abs 11n Limit" for 40 MHz transmit spectrum mask now in line with IEEE, see "Transmit Spectrum Mask OFDM, Default Masks"
