CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:UTEPower <UTEPower>

Sets the type of limits to be applied for the unused tone error of HE TB PPDU.

Parameters:
<UTEPower>
HIGH | LOW

HIGH: used limits are according to the transmit power larger than the maximum power of MCS 7

LOW: used limits are according to the transmit power less than or equal to the maximum power of MCS 7

See "Specifying Modulation Limits (OFDM)"
