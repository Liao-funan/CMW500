CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:RFSettings:FREQuency:BAND <FreqBand>

Selects the frequency band.

Parameters:
<FreqBand>
B24Ghz | B5GHz | B4GHz

B24Ghz: 2.4 GHz band

B4GHz: 4 GHz band

B5GHz: 5 GHz band

*RST:B24G
