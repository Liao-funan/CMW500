V3.2.40 (Compared to V3.2.20)
New features:
  • Support for IEEE 802.11p signals
    –20 MHz, 10 MHz, and 5 MHz channel bandwidth (full-, half-, and quarter-clocked operation)
    –Multiple simultaneous measurements: power vs. time, EVM vs. symbol, EVM vs. carrier, I/Q constellation, spectrum flatness, transmit spectrum mask, TX measurement (scalar)
    –Limit checks for transmitter constellation error, transmit center frequency error, symbol clock frequency error, spectrum flatness and transmit spectrum mask, see "Limit Settings"
