Switched MIMO Measurements

In switched MIMO mode, the R&S CMW records up to 8 DUT (transmit) antennas, simultaneously connected to different RF input ports. This mode allows you to decode the spatial streams of a MIMO signal and to measure the modulation accuracy according to IEEE Std 802.11-2012 (section 20.3.20.7).

However, the antenna signals cannot be recorded in parallel. The measurement application switches between the different antennas in a round-robin fashion. The DUT has to transmit the same burst repeatedly, retriggering the measurement with every burst.

After each antenna cycle (i.e. after all antenna signals are recorded once), the following actions are performed:
  • The spatial streams are decoded.
  • The modulation quality results are updated.
  • The statistic counter is incremented.
  • MIMO feature requires option R&S CMW-KM653.
  • On the R&S CMW100, switched MIMO is available for IEEE 802.11n, 802.11ac, and 802.11ax signals. IEEE 802.11ax requires also an R&S CMW with TRX160.
  • On other instruments, switched MIMO is limited to IEEE 802.11n signals and requires two basic frontends.
