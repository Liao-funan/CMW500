CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIST:SEGMent<Seg>:MODulation <Statistics>, <EVMEnable>

Defines the statistical length for AVERage, MAXimum and SDEViation calculation and enables the calculation of the different modulation results in segment no. <Seg>; see "Multi-Evaluation List Mode".

Suffix: 
<Seg>
1..100

The segment number must not exceed the total number of segments measured (see CONFigure:​WLAN:​MEAS<i>:​MEValuation:​LIST:​COUNt).

Parameters:
<Statistics>

The statistical length is limited by the length of the segment (see CONFigure:​WLAN:​MEAS<i>:​MEValuation:​LIST:​SEGMent<Seg>:​SETup).

Range: 1  to  1000
*RST:1
<EVMEnable>
OFF | ON

OFF: Disable measurement

ON: Enable measurement

*RST:ON
Example: 

See "Using WLAN List Mode"

Firmware/Software: 

V1.0.10.50

Options: R&S CMW-KM012
