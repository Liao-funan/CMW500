CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:RESult:MSCalar <ModEnable>

Enables or disables the evaluation of results and shows or hides the modulation scalar view.

Parameters:
<ModEnable>
OFF | ON

OFF: Do not evaluate results, hide the view

ON: Evaluate results and show the view

*RST:ON
Firmware/Software: 

V1.0.10.50

Manual operation: See "Assign Views"
