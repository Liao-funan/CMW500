Modulation Limits 802.11a/g/p OFDM

IEEE defines EVM limits from -25 dB to -5 dB, depending on the modulation type and coding rate. Center frequency and symbol clock tolerance are specified in ppm of the carrier frequency. For 802.11p, they depend on the frequency band and the channel width.
