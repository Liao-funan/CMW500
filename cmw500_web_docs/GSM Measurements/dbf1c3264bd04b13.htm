Test Setup

The downlink RF generator signal of the R&S CMW is fed to the input of the DUT. The R&S CMW measures the uplink signal. Most conveniently, a bi-directional connection with a single coax cable is used. Connect the DUT to one of the bidirectional RF connectors on the front panel of the R&S CMW.
