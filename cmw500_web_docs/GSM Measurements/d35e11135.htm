CONFigure:GSM:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:MOEXception <MeasOnException>

Specifies whether measurement results that the R&S CMW identifies as faulty or inaccurate are rejected.

Parameters:
<MeasOnException>
ON | OFF

ON: Results are never rejected

OFF: Faulty results are rejected

*RST:OFF
