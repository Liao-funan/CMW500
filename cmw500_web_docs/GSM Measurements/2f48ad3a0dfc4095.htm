CONFigure:GSM:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:SSWitching:PHMode <PeakHoldMode>

Specifies how the peak hold mode is used for the spectrum switching results in frequency domain (bar graphs) and in time domain.

Parameters:
<PeakHoldMode>
PHOL | SCO

PHOL: Frequency and time: peak hold

SCO: Frequency: stat. count, time: current

*RST:PHOL
