Speed Considerations
The following measurement settings provide additional results but can slow down the measurement:
  • Evaluation of the spectrum vs. time measurements
  • Evaluation of the AM-PM delay for polar modulators
  • Access burst search
The following settings improve the accuracy of the results but require additional processing time:
  • Measurement of 8PSK or 16-QAM-modulated bursts in "Data Compensated" reference power mode.
