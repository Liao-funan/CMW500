CONFigure:GSM:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:TSEQuence <TSC>

Selects the training sequence of the analyzed bursts.

For the combined signal path scenario, use CONFigure:​GSM:​SIGN<i>:​CELL:​BCC.

Parameters:
<TSC>
OFF | TSC0 | TSC1 | TSC2 | TSC3 | TSC4 | TSC5 | TSC6 | TSC7 | TSCA | DUMM

OFF: Analyze all bursts, irrespective of their training sequence

TSC0 ... TSC7:Analyze bursts with a particular GSM training sequence

TSCA: Analyze bursts with any of the GSM training sequences TSC0 to TSC7

DUMMY: Analyze GSM-specific dummy bursts

*RST:TSC0
