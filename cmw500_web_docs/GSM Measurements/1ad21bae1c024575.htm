CONFigure:GSM:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:FCRange <Mode>

Selects the width of the frequency range that the R&S CMW analyzes to establish time-synchronization with the received signal.

Parameters:
<Mode>
NORMal | WIDE

NORMal: Normal frequency range

WIDE: Wide frequency range

*RST:NORM
