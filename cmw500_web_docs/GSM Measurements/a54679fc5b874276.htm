Measuring an UL GSM Signal
After connecting your GSM mobile phone to the R&S CMW as shown above, you have to adjust the following analyzer settings to the properties of the analyzed UL GSM signal:
  • The analyzer frequency
  • The expected nominal power and (optional) a user margin and external attenuation
    Recommended values: "Expected Nominal Power" = average power of the UE signal; "User Margin" = + 5 dB (for 8PSK-modulated bursts) and +7.5 dB (for 16-QAM-modulated bursts).

The R&S CMW uses an RF power trigger and synchronizes to the incoming bursts and decode the signal.
