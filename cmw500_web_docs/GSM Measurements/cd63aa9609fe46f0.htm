General Description

The GSM multi-evaluation measurement (option R&S CMW-KM200) captures an uplink (UL) GSM signal and provides the TX measurement results over a series of consecutive slots. With an additional ARB generator, you can also execute bit error rate measurements (RX tests).

The following sections describe how to perform and configure the measurement.
