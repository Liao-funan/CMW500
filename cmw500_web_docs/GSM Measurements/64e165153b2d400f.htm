CONFigure:GSM:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:HDALevel <HighDynAssLevel>

Defines a signal level relative to the "Expected Nominal Power" (CONFigure:​GSM:​MEAS<i>:​RFSettings:​ENPower) where the two results obtained in a two stage measurement are joined.

Parameters:
<HighDynAssLevel>
Range: -60 dB  to  -10 dB
*RST:-50 dB

Additional parameters: OFF | ON (disables | enables two-shot measurement)

See "Selecting Specific Burst Types"
