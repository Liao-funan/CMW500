TRIGger:BASE:EXTA:SLOPe <Slope>
TRIGger:BASE:EXTB:SLOPe <Slope>

Specifies whether the rising edge or the falling edge of the trigger pulse is generated at the trigger event.

The setting applies to output trigger signals provided at the TRIG A (EXTA) or TRIG B (EXTB) connector.

Parameters:
<Slope>
REDGe | FEDGe

REDGe: Rising edge

FEDGe: Falling edge

*RST:REDG
Example: TRIGger:BASE:EXTB:SLOPe FEDGe

Generate a falling edge for TRIG B.

Firmware/Software: 

V3.0.10
