Trigger Settings

The following commands configure the connectors TRIG A and TRIG B on the rear panel of the instrument. They also allow you to initiate the generation of a "User Initiated Trigger" signal.

The settings are also available in the "Misc > Trigger" section of the "Setup" dialog.

Contents of this chapter

The commands in this section belong to the R&S CMW base system. They are not related to specific firmware applications.

Most of the R&S CMW measurement firmware applications provide their own, specific trigger settings. For details, refer to the documentation of the TRIGger... subsystems in the firmware application documentation.
