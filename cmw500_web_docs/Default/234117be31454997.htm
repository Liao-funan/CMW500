LAN SWITCH R&S CMW-B661H (Optional)

The LAN switch R&S CMW-B661H provides up to two external ports, accessible via eight-pin RJ45 connectors. The ports provide access to the internal IP network of the instrument (CMW subnet), for example for logging of signaling messages.

The switch supports 1 Gbit/s and 10 Gbit/s. The used mode is indicated by the right LED (green on = 1 Gbit/s, amber on = 10 Gbit/s). The left LED indicates the link activity (green blinking).

In 10 Gbit/s mode, the maximum allowed length of the LAN cable is 5 m.

The number of available external ports depends on the installed DAU, as follows.
