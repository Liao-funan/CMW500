RTT with Pareto Jitter Distribution

Modeling of the delay jitter in a real network is still a matter of research. However, some papers claim that the pareto distribution is suitable to model the delay jitter of the Internet.

A delay of 70 ms is configured and also a jitter of 10 ms with a pareto distribution.
