CONFigure:DATA:CONTrol:IMS2:UPDate:EVS:COMMon:BITRate:RANGe <BitrateLower>, <BitrateHigher>

Selects the bit-rate range supported in the EVS primary mode, for a call update and common configuration.

Parameters:
<BitrateLower>
R59 | R72 | R80 | R96 | R132 | R164 | R244 | R320 | R480 | R640 | R960 | R1280

Lower end of the range, 5.9 kbit/s to 128 kbit/s

<BitrateHigher>
R59 | R72 | R80 | R96 | R132 | R164 | R244 | R320 | R480 | R640 | R960 | R1280

Upper end of the range, 5.9 kbit/s to 128 kbit/s
