SOURce:GPRF:GEN<i>:ARB:FOFFset 
<FrequencyOffset>
Sets/gets the frequency offset to be imposed at the baseband during ARB generation. In the standalone scenario, the offset results in an equivalent frequency offset at the RF.
Parameters:
<FrequencyOffset>
Range: 
-40E+6  to  +40E+6
*RST:
1.2E+9 Hz
Default unit: 
Hz
Firmware/Software: 
V3.0.10
Manual operation: 
See 
"Baseband Configuration > ARB > Frequency Offset"
