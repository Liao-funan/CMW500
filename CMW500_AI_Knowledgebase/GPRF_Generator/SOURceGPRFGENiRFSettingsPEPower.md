SOURce:GPRF:GEN<i>:RFSettings:PEPower? 
Queries the peak envelope power for single-tone and dual-tone signals.
Return values: 
<PeakEnvelopePow>
Range: 
-130 dBm to 0 dBm at RF 1 COM and RF 2 COM, -120 dBm to 13 dBm at RF 1 OUT; notice the ranges quoted in the data sheet
Increment: 
0.01 dB
Default unit: 
dBm

See 
"Generating a CW Signal"
Usage: 
Query only
Firmware/Software: 
V1.0.4.11

See 
"Level (RMS)"
