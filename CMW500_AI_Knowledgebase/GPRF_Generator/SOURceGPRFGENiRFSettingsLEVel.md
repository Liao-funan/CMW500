SOURce:GPRF:GEN<i>:RFSettings:LEVel 
<Level>
Sets the base RMS level of the constant-frequency RF generator.
Parameters:
<Level>
Range: 
-130 dBm to 0 dBm at RF 1 COM and RF 2 COM, -120 dBm to 13 dBm at RF 1 OUT; notice the ranges quoted in the data sheet
Increment: 
0.01 dB
*RST:
-30 dBm
Default unit: 
dBm
