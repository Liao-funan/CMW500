ROUTe:WLAN:MEAS<i>:SCENario:CSPath 
<Master>
Activates the combined signal path scenario and selects the master application. The master controls the signal routing and analyzer settings while the combined signal path scenario is active.
Parameters:
<Master>
String parameter selecting the master application
e.g. 
'WLAN Sig1'
 or 
'WLAN Sig2'

"Scenario = Combined Signal Path"
