CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:ISIGnal:CDIStance 
<ChannelDistance>
Configures the distance between the center frequencies of the two 80-MHz segments of an 802.11ac 80+80 MHz signal.
On R&S
 
CMW500/2xx with BB Meas, the distance is fixed (contiguous segments).
On R&S
 
CMW100/CMW with MUA, the distance is configurable.
Parameters:
<ChannelDistance>
Range: 
80 MHz  to  940 MHz
Increment: 
5 MHz
*RST:
80 MHz
Default unit: 
MHz
Firmware/Software: 
V3.5.121, V3.7.10 maximum reduced
Options: 
R&S CMW-KM656
Manual operation: 
See 
"Ch. Distance"
