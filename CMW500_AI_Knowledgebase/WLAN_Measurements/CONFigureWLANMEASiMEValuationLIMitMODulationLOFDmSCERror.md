CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:MODulation:LOFDm:SCERror 
<ClockError>
Defines and activates an upper limit for the symbol clock error (802.11a/g, OFDM).
Parameters:
<ClockError>
Range: 
0 ppm  to  100 ppm
*RST:
25 ppm
Default unit: 
ppm
Additional parameters: OFF | ON (disables | enables the limit check)

"Specifying Modulation Limits (OFDM)"
