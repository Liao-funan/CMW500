CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:MODulation:DSSS:IQOFfset 
<IQOffset>
Defines and activates an upper limit for the I/Q origin offset (transmission scheme DSSS).
Parameters:
<IQOffset>
Range: 
-100 dB  to  0 dB
*RST:
-15 dB
Default unit: 
dB
Additional parameters: OFF | ON (disables | enables the limit check)

"Specifying Limits (DSSS)"
