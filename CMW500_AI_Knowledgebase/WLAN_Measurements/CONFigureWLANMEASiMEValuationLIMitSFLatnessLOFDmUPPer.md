CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:SFLatness:LOFDm:UPPer 
<Upper>
Defines an upper limit for the spectrum flatness of 802.11a/g OFDM signals.
The upper limit must be larger than the lower limits.
Parameters:
<Upper>
Range: 
-4 dB  to  20 dB
*RST:
4 dB
Default unit: 
dB
