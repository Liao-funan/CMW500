Spectrum Flatness Limits: 802.11ax

CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:SFLatness:HEOFdm:BW<bandwidth>:UPPer
CONFigure:WLAN:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:SFLatness:HEOFdm:BW<bandwidth>:UPPer 
<Upper>
Defines an upper limit for the spectrum flatness of 802.11ax signals with the specified <bandwidth>.
The upper limit must be larger than the lower limits.
Suffix: 
<bandwidth>
20, 40, 80, 160
Parameters:
<Upper>
Range: 
-4 dB  to  20 dB
*RST:
4 dB
Default unit: 
dB
Example: 
See 
"Specifying Spectrum Flatness Limits (OFDM)"
