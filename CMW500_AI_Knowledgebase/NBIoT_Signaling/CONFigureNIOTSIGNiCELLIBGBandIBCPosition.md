Specifies the number of the LTE RB located at the NB-IoT channel position, for the in-band operation mode.
Only specific values within the stated range are allowed, depending on the LTE channel BW, see 
Table "LTE RB indices allowed for NB-IoT in-band operation, depending on LTE cell BW"
