CONFigure:GPRF:MEAS<i>:IQVSlot:FELimit 
<Limit>
Defines a minimum average signal level relative to the expected nominal power. The setting applies to all measurement steps considered for the calculation of the average frequency per subsweep and the overall frequency error.
Parameters:
<Limit>
Range: 
-100 dB  to  -30 dB
*RST:
-100 dB
Default unit: 
dB
