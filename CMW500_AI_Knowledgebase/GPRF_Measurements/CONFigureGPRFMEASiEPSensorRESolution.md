CONFigure:GPRF:MEAS<i>:EPSensor:RESolution 
<Resolution>
Defines the number of digits of the power results in the measurement dialog. This command does not affect the remote control results.
Parameters:

 PD1 |
 
 PD2 |
 
 PD3

PD1:

PD3:
 0.001
