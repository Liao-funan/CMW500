CONFigure:GPRF:MEAS<i>:POWer:PSET:SLENgth:ALL 
<StepLength>...
Selects the time between the beginning of two consecutive measured power steps for all parameter sets.
Parameters:
<StepLength>
Comma-separated list of 32 values, for parameter set no. 0 to 31
Range: 
50E-6 s  to  1 s
*RST:
576.9230769E-6 s (1 GSM timeslot)
Default unit: 
s
