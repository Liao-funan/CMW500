<PCCBBBoard>, <RXConnector>, <RXConverter>, <PCCTX1Connector>, <PCCTX1Converter>, <PCCIQ1Connector>, <PCCTX2Connector>, <PCCTX2Converter>, <PCCIQ2Connector>, <SCC1BBBoard>, <SCC1TX1Conn>, <SCC1TX1Conv>, <SCC1IQ1Conn>, <SCC1TX2Conn>, <SCC1TX2Conv>, <SCC1IQ2Conn>, <SCC2BBBoard>, <SCC2TX1Conn>, <SCC2TX1Conv>, <SCC2IQ1Conn>, <SCC2TX2Conn>, <SCC2TX2Conv>, <SCC2IQ2Conn>, <SCC3BBBoard>, <SCC3TX1Conn>, <SCC3TX1Conv>, <SCC3IQ1Conn>, <SCC3TX2Conn>, <SCC3TX2Conv>, <SCC3IQ2Conn>

For possible parameter values, see 
"Values for Signal Path Selection"
.
