CONFigure:WCDMa:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:RESult:EVMagnitude 
<EnableEVM>
Enables or disables the evaluation of results and shows or hides the error vector magnitude view in the multi-evaluation measurement.
Parameters:
<EnableEVM>
OFF |
 
 ON
OFF:
 Do not evaluate results, hide the view
ON:
 Evaluate results and show the view
