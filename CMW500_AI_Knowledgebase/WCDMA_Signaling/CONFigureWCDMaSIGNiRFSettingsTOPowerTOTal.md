CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:RFSettings:TOPower:TOTal? 
Queries the sum of the output channel power (Ior) and the AWGN power (Ioc).
For scenarios with multi-carrier, the result indicates the sum of the Ior and Ioc values of all carriers.
Return values: 
<CombTotOutPwr>
Default unit: 
dBm
Example: 
See 
"Specifying General Settings"
Usage: 
Query only
Firmware/Software: 
V2.1.30

See
