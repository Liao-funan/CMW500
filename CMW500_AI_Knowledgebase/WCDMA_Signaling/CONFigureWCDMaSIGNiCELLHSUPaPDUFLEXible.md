CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CELL:HSUPa:PDU:FLEXible 
<FlexibleMax>
Enables and selects the maximum RLC PDU size to be signaled to the UE to configure its flexible UL RLC PDU for dual uplink carrier connections.
Parameters:
<FlexibleMax>
Range: 
16  to  12.04E+3
Increment: 
8
*RST:
12.04E+3
Additional ON/OFF enables/disables flexible PDU size.

"Configuring HSUPA Settings"

See
