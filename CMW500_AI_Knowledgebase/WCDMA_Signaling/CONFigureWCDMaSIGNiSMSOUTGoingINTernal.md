CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:SMS:OUTGoing:INTernal 
<SMSInternal>
Defines the message text for SMS messages to be sent to the UE. It is encoded as 7-bit ASCII text.
Parameters:
<SMSInternal>
String with up to 800 characters
*RST:
"R&S Short Message Service Text. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. THE QUICK BROWN FOX JUMPS OVER THE LAZY DOG. 0123456789 !"#\*%+-/()<>?=;@$,"
