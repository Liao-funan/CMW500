CONFigure:WCDMa:SIGN<i>:CELL:TOUT:ATOFfset 
<Offset>
Specifies a delay value, used by the RRC for calculation of the activation time in peer messages.
Low values correspond to fast signaling, high values to slow signaling.

<Offset>
Range: 
0  to  10
