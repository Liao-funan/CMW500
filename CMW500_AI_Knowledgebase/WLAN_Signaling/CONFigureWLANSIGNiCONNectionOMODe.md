CONFigure:WLAN:SIGN<i>:CONNection:OMODe 
<mode>
Selects the operation mode, that is the type of WLAN entity simulated by the WLAN signaling application.
Parameters:
<mode>
AP |
 
 STATion |
 
 IBSS |
 
 WDIRect |
 
 HSPot2
AP
: access point in infrastructure mode
STATion
: WLAN station
IBSS
: WLAN station in IBSS (ad hoc) mode
WDIRect
: Wi-Fi Direct group owner
HSPot2
: WiFi Hotspot 2.0 access point
*RST:
AP
