CONFigure:WLAN:SIGN<i>:CONNection:RXFilter 
<FilterType>
Selects the type of inter-symbol interference filter to be used for DSSS CCK signals with a data rate of 5.5 Mbit/s or 11 Mbit/s.
Parameters:
<FilterType>
DEFault |
 
 ALTernative |
 
 AUTO
DEFault and ALTernative are predefined static filters.
AUTO is an adaptive filter.
