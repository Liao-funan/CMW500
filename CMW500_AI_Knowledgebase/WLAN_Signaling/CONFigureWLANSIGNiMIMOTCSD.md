CONFigure:WLAN:SIGN<i>:MIMO:TCSD 
<csd1>, <csd2>
Configures the cyclic shift diversity for the MIMO scenario. The setting is relevant for spatial multiplexing and STBC.
Parameters:
<csd1>
CSD for TX1

-800 ns  to  800 ns
*RST:
0 ns
Default unit: 
ns
<csd2>

Range: 
-800 ns  to  800 ns
*RST:
0 ns

ns
