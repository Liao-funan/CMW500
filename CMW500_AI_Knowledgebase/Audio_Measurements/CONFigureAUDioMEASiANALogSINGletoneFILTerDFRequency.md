<DistortionFreq>
Specifies the distortion frequency for single tone measurements (fundamental frequency of the AF signal).
The setting command is only relevant if the measurement is not coupled to an audio generator. Otherwise the distortion frequency is set automatically according to the generator settings.
Parameters:
<DistortionFreq>
Range: 
1 Hz  to  10.5E+3 Hz
*RST:
1000 Hz
Default unit: 
Hz

See 
"Single Tone Measurement"

V3.2.10
Manual operation: 
See 
"Filter > Distortion Frequency"
