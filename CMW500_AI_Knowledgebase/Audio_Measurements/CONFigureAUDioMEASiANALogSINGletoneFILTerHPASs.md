Configures the cutoff frequency of the highpass filter for single tone measurements.
Parameters:
<Frequency>
OFF |
 
 H6 |
 
 H50 |
 
 H300
OFF:
 highpass filter disabled
H6 | H50 | H300:
 cutoff frequency 6 Hz, 50 Hz or 300 Hz
*RST:
ANALog = OFF, DIGital = H6
