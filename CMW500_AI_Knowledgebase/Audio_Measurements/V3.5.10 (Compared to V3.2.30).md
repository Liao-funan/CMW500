V3.5.10 (Compared to V3.2.30)
New features:
Speech analysis enhancements:
–
Changed coupling between speech codec and signaling application. The setting "Enable Speech Codec" is removed from the signaling applications. The coupled application is now selected in the audio measurement settings, see 
"Speech Analysis Settings"
.
–
Event log and information provided by coupled master application, see 
"Speech Analysis Tab"
–
The clock of the audio board can be adjusted
Scenario selection improvements:
–
New commands, one command per scenario, see 
"General Settings"
–
The scenario is selectable per audio application instance.
Support of 
STATus:OPERation
 registers, with the following register paths:
–
STATus:OPERation:TASK:A:AUDio:GENerator<i>:ANALog | DIGital
–
STATus:OPERation:TASK:A:AUDio:MEASurement<i>:ANALog | DIGital
