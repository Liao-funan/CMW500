CONFigure:GSM:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:ROTation:IQ 
<Rotation>
Specifies whether the rotation of the 8PSK and 16-QAM symbols is subtracted off before the symbols are displayed in the constellation diagram.
Parameters:
<Rotation>
P38 |
 
 P38R

 Rotation removed
