CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:RFSettings:CHCCombined:TCH:CSWitched 
<Channel>, <Timeslot>, <PCL>
Sets/changes the GSM channel number, timeslot, and PCL. All parameters can be changed during a connection.
Parameters:
<Channel>
Range: 
0 to 124, 940 to 1023
*RST:
62
<Timeslot>
Range: 
1  to  7
*RST:
3
<PCL>
Range: 
0  to  31
*RST:
10
