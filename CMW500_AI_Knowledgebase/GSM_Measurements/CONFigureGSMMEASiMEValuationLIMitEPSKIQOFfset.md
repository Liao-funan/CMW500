CONFigure:GSM:MEAS<i>:MEValuation:LIMit:EPSK:IQOFfset 
<Limit>, <StatCurrent>, <StatAverage>, <StatMax>

-100 dB  to  0 dB
*RST:
-30 dB

dB

ON |
 
 OFF
