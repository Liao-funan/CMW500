<TXantennaSynch>
Specifies via which antenna the synchronization signals are transferred.
Parameters:
<TXantennaSynch>
ALL |
 
 A1 |
 
 A2
ALL
: All antennas (SISO antenna or both MIMO 2x2 antennas)
A1
: Antenna 1 (SISO antenna or first MIMO 2x2 TX antenna)
A2
: Antenna 2 (second MIMO 2x2 TX antenna)
