SENSe:GSM:SIGN<i>:RREPort:NSRQam<ModOrder>:MBEP? 
Returns the "Mean BEP", reported by the MS as dimensionless index for a 16-QAM or 32-QAM modulated DL signal with normal symbol rate (NSR).
Suffix: 
<ModOrder>
16,32
Selects the modulation scheme: 16-QAM or 32-QAM
Return values: 
<MeanBEP>
Range: 
0  to  31

"Retrieving PS Receiver Reports"
