CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:CELL:RESelection:QUALity:RXLevmin:EUTRan 
<Qrxlevmin>
Defines the minimum RX level at a UE antenna required for access to the LTE cell. This parameter is transmitted via BCCH.
Parameters:
<Qrxlevmin>
Range: 
-140 dBm  to  -78 dBm
Increment: 
2 dB
*RST:
-132 dBm

See 
"Configuring Neighbor Cell and Reselection Parameters"

V3.2.20

R&S CMW-KS210

See 
"E-UTRAN Q RxLevMin"
