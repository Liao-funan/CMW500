CONFigure:GSM:SIGN<i>:CELL:RESelection:QUALity:RXLevmin:UTRan 
<Qrxlevmin>
Defines the minimum RX level at a UE antenna required for access to the UMTS cell. This parameter is transmitted via BCCH.
Parameters:
<Qrxlevmin>
Range: 
-119 dBm  to  -57 dBm
Increment: 
2 dB
*RST:
-115 dBm
Example: 
See 
"Configuring Neighbor Cell and Reselection Parameters"
Firmware/Software: 
V3.2.20
Options: 
R&S CMW-KS210
Manual operation: 
See 
"UTRAN Q RxLevMin"
