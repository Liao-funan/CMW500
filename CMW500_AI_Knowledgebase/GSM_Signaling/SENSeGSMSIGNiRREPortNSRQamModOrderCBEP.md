SENSe:GSM:SIGN<i>:RREPort:NSRQam<ModOrder>:CBEP? 
Returns the "CV BEP", reported by the MS as dimensionless index for a 16-QAM or 32-QAM modulated DL signal with normal symbol rate (NSR).
Suffix: 
<ModOrder>
16,32
Selects the modulation scheme: 16-QAM or 32-QAM
Return values: 
<CV_BEP>
Range: 
0  to  7
