CONFigure:NIOT:MEAS<i>:PRACh:MODulation:EWLength 
<LengthFormat0>, <LengthFormat1>

<LengthFormat0>

Range:

*RST:
110
<LengthFormat1>

Range: 
1  to  512
*RST:
494
