CONFigure:NIOT:MEAS<i>:PRACh:LIMit:FERRor 
<FrequencyError>
Defines an upper limit for the carrier frequency error.
Parameters:
<FrequencyError>
Range: 
0 ppm  to  1000 ppm
*RST:
0.1 ppm, ON
Additional parameters: OFF | ON (disables the limit check | enables the limit check using the previous/default limit values)
