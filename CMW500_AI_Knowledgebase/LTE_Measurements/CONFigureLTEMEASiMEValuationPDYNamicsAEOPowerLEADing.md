<Leading>
Shifts the beginning of the evaluation period for OFF power measurements.
Parameters:
<Leading>
Positive values reduce the evaluation period (starts later).
Negative values increase the evaluation period (starts earlier).
Range: 
-1000 Ts  to  1000 Ts
*RST:
0 Ts

Ts
